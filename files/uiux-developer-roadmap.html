What is a UI/UX Developer?

Someone who designs interfaces, understands the people using them, and can build the real thing — sitting at the intersection of design and frontend engineering. This roadmap is built for four kinds of learner:

BEGINNER

Starting from zero

No design or code background yet. Begin at Phase 01 and move through in order.

INTERMEDIATE

Some Figma or code

Comfortable with Figma, HTML/CSS, or basic JavaScript. Skip ahead to the phase that matches what you already know.

ADVANCED

Going professional

Ready for design systems, accessibility, performance, and product thinking. Use this as a working reference.

CAREER SWITCHER

Changing tracks

Coming from another field entirely. Follow the full path and lean on the Career section for interview prep.
